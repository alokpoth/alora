Frequently Asked Questions
What is Alora?
Alora is a high-speed intelligence platform built for coding, academic research, generative image synthesis, and Bengali language conversational mastery.
How does the fair usage quota work?
Each user plan includes a rolling window (e.g. Free: 10 messages / 3 hours, Pro: 30 / 3 hours, Max: 50 / 1 hour). When your limit is reached, your quota automatically resets as the window expires.
How can I redeem a code?
Navigate to the Redeem Code page from Settings, enter your promo code, and your account will immediately upgrade to Pro or Max.
Are Admin accounts rate-limited?
No. Verified admin accounts enjoy unlimited messages, unrestricted image generation, and instant access to all AI models without queuing or rate limits.
Can I use Alora offline?
Yes! All your previous chats, drafts, and preferences are cached locally using IndexedDB and LocalStorage, allowing you to browse and review conversations even with no network connection.
How do I change themes or speech audio?
You can adjust the theme from Settings > Color Theme (OLED, Deep Sapphire, Light, Eye Care) and configure speech audio, volume, and tones from Settings > Sound Settings.
